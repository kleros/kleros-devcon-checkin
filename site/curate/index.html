      Privacy

      If you play with Telegram, Cooperative Kleros keeps your Telegram user ID (a number) and your Telegram username (if you have one) in a private list, to send you Telegram messages through the Kleros Booth bot on how to take part in real contests across Kleros apps. Booth staff can use your username to find your game tries and reset them. We keep both until you send /stop to the bot, which deletes them, or ask us to delete them: ask at the booth. Your game tries are stored under a scrambled code, in a separate list, never next to your Telegram ID or username.

      If you leave an email or @Telegram to unlock merch, Cooperative Kleros keeps it in a private list for the Kleros newsletter, and for curator info only if you tick that box. A Telegram handle gets one message with the newsletter channel link, nothing else. We keep it until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it: ask at the booth. Your badge is stored only as a scrambled code, in a separate list, never next to your contact.

      How Kleros Curate works

      1. Apps rely on lists: places, products, facts.
      2. Anyone can add an entry, with a deposit.
      3. Anyone can challenge an entry that breaks the rules.
      4. Kleros jurors decide. The winner takes the deposit.

      Here: Wrong is a challenge. 100 points for each right answer, 10 for trying. Teal at 6 right, Purple at 8, Gold for 10 out of 10.
